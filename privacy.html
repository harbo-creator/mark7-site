Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mark 7 Industries LLC ("we," "us") handles information in connection with our website and the apps we publish (together, the "Services"). If an individual app has its own privacy notice, that notice applies in addition to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. For example, when you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • Data stored in our apps. Unless an app's description says otherwise, the information you enter into our apps is stored on your device or in your own iCloud account, not on our servers.
  • Diagnostics. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers through your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

How we use information

We use information only to provide and improve the Services, respond to your requests and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Third-party services

If an app uses a third-party service (for example, analytics or cloud storage), that app's App Store listing and in-app notice will identify it. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app. To request deletion of any information you have sent us directly, such as support emails, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
